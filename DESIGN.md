# D place

A knowledge shop that lives inside a wallet. Paper, stamp, coin.

## Identity

D place is a stall, not a campus. Listings sit on a warm counter. NIM is the coin that changes hands. The name is two words, lowercase p: **D place**.

Audience: people already in Nimiq Pay who want to buy a skill, sell one, or fund a job.

## Personality

Direct. Slightly ink-stained. No hype. Money is spoken as NIM amounts, not as "seamless earning".

## Palette

- Paper `#F3E6C8` page ground (library stock, not tech white)
- Ink `#1A1714` text and chrome
- Gold `#C8920A` NIM, funded prizes, primary money actions
- Stamp `#7A1F2B` identity mark, errors, reports
- Neutral: ink at 62% for secondary text, paper darkened to `#E7D7B0` for wells

Gold is the only accent. Stamp red is for the D mark and destructive/report paths, not decoration.

## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces** (optical size). A catalog serif; knowledge is the product.
- UI: **Source Sans 3**. Clear at 16px on a phone in a WebView.

## Motif

A rectangular **D** stamp in stamp red, like a library date stamp. Used once in the header, small. Not a grid, not a glow.

## Surfaces

8px radius on fields and cards. 2px on the stamp. Buttons are not pills. Most surfaces are flat; the checkout sheet lifts with a tight shadow because it is a payment, not a card.

## Theme

Light by default. This is a reading product. No dark mode in MVP.
