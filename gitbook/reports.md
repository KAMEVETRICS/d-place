# Reports

On a listing or bounty, **Send report** (reason 8–500 characters). You must be signed in with a username.

A listing report sets that listing to `suspended`. It leaves Shop and Listings. Library entitlements for people who already paid still work.

Bounty reports are stored. They do not auto-cancel a funded prize.

Reports are for the operator to read in the database. There is no public moderation queue in this version.
